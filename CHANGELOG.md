# Changelog

Releases from `0.8.0` onwards are generated automatically by `release-please`
from conventional commits. See [docs/RELEASING.md](docs/RELEASING.md).

Entries up to and including `0.7.0` were hand-written, migrated here from the
retired `GET /changelog` endpoint and preserved verbatim as the historical
record.

## 0.7.0 (2026-05-21)

- `/subjects/{subject}` now exposes `ks4ProgrammeFactors.childSubject` for
  subjects split into child subjects at KS4 (currently science → biology,
  chemistry, combined-science, physics)
- Removed the per-sequence `ks4Options` field from `sequenceSlugs[]` in
  `/subjects`, `/subjects/{subject}`, and `/subjects/{subject}/sequences`
  responses; the variant is still encoded in the sequenceSlug suffix

## 0.6.0 (2026-02-19)

- Change to how blocked content is handled, using BAD_REQUEST & `.data.cause`
  instead of 451

## 0.5.0 (2025-03-06)

- PPTX used for slideDeck assets
- All video assets now fully downloadable in mp4 format
- New `/threads/*` endpoints

## 0.4.0 (2025-02-07)

- Added `/sequences/*` and `/subjects/*` endpoints, and add support for unit
  optionality

## 0.3.0 (2024-10-21)

- Add `attribution` to asset endpoints

## 0.2.0 (2024-06-07)

- Quiz and questions now include starterQuiz and exitQuiz as keys to the
  results, and separate the questions into their own array

## 0.1.1 (2024-04-17)

- Change `slug` and `title` to `unitSlug` and `unitTitle`, or `lessonSlug` etc
- `/search/lessons/text-similarity` changed to `/search/lessons`

## 0.1.0 (2024-03-19)

- Initial beta release
